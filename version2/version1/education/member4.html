Project Highlights

Bond Auction Predictor

Analysed 140+ Bank of Tanzania bond auctions from 2021 to 2026 in Python. Bond tenor turned out to be the dominant yield driver (r = 0.704), while investor competition had no real effect (r = −0.023).

I then built a linear regression model (R² = 0.58, MAE 1.47%) to predict future clearing yields and mapped Tanzania’s full monetary policy cycle across all tenors.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Jupyter, Scikit-learn, Seaborn

Monte Carlo Stock Price Predictor

A modular Monte Carlo stock price simulation engine with a Streamlit dashboard. It runs up to 10,000 independent price paths over two years using Geometric Brownian Motion with Itô’s correction.

The engine reports key risk metrics and draws interactive charts of the simulated path distribution.

Tools: Python, NumPy, Streamlit
